# Geography Deep Dive: Regional Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail performance across regions, cities, city tiers, and store formats to understand geographic growth, customer acquisition, revenue concentration, and market performance.

## Business Problem

The business needed to understand whether growth was broad-based geographically, which locations and store formats were performing strongly, where underperformance existed, and how revenue concentration could affect geographic strategy.

## Tools & Technologies

- Databricks
- SQL
- Power BI

## Key Work Performed

- Analyzed performance across all five regions.
- Reviewed city-level and city-tier performance.
- Compared Fresh, Supermarket, and Express store formats.
- Analyzed new-customer growth across geographies.
- Identified revenue concentration among top cities.
- Reviewed product and category demand patterns by geography.
- Identified underperforming cities and store formats.

## Key Insights

- National growth was approximately 20–26% YoY across all five regions.
- West and South showed 25%+ growth.
- Fresh format showed approximately 28–32% growth.
- The top 15 cities contributed 86% of revenue.
- Tier 1 growth was 27.7%, Tier 2 was 22.7%, and Tier 3 was 17.2%.
- The top five cities contributed 39% of revenue.
- Express performance was broadly 15–20%, compared with 25–30% for Fresh and Supermarket.
- New-customer growth was 55–65% across the analyzed markets.

## Business Impact

The analysis provides a geographic performance view that can support differentiated strategies across regions, cities, tiers, and store formats. Strong-performing areas can be studied for expansion, while weaker location-format combinations can be reviewed for improvement.
